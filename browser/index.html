Model limits

Move a drive electrode and the same control signal can produce a different rotation. EmulateQPU-4Q connects layout and electromagnetic extraction to a coupled transmon model and pulse calibration. Predicting a fabricated chip also requires measured calibration.

Circuit and readout assumptions

Each wire keeps |0⟩, |1⟩ and |2⟩. Finite local drives can populate |2⟩. CX acts as identity when either of its wires occupies |2⟩. Independent relaxation and dephasing act after each column for its full duration, including on idle wires. Ideal level-resolving readout records |2⟩ as L. Pulse timings are illustrative.

Where QuTiP and IQM Pulse enter

The circuit result uses JavaScript. The isolated pulse experiment runs QuTiP 4.7.0 in your browser and uses waveform samples generated by IQM Pulse 16.0.0. It starts from a fresh state. The selected drive contributes its nominal angle, duration and phase. The public source includes native Python reproduction.
